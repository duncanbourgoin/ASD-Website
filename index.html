Core Capabilities

Disciplined Project Execution Across the Development Lifecycle

We bridge the gap between commercial real estate ownership, utility grid engineering, and institutional energy finance—de-risking solar assets from initial feasibility through construction readiness.

1. Site Origination & Control

Data-driven identification of high-yield commercial rooftops and proximate greenfield parcels inside advantaged utility territories.

  • Solar irradiance & structural roof modeling
  • Long-term roof & ground lease structuring
  • Title review, ALTA surveys & site diligence

2. Interconnection & Engineering

Comprehensive management of utility queue applications, hosting capacity analysis, and municipal permitting to secure Notice to Proceed (NTP).

  • Feeder hosting capacity & transformer analysis
  • Utility interconnection agreements (ISA)
  • AHJ zoning, structural PE stamps & environmental

3. Offtake, Incentives & Finance

Optimization of state renewable energy credit (REC) contracts, federal tax credit monetization, and commercial power purchase agreements.

  • State REC program bidding & compliance (e.g., Illinois Shines)
  • Federal ITC & energy community bonus optimization
  • BTM Power Purchase Agreements & EPC coordination

Partnership Criteria

What We Look For in Real Estate & Industrial Partners

Whether you own a single manufacturing facility or manage a multi-state logistics portfolio, American Solar Development provides transparent, institutional execution with zero upfront capital required from the property owner.

Value to Property Owners

Generate predictable 20-to-25-year passive roof lease income or lock in below-market fixed electricity rates while hedging against utility capacity charges.

01 / Industrial & Logistics Rooftops

40,000+ Sq. Ft. Unobstructed

Flat or low-slope commercial roofs (TPO, EPDM, PVC, or standing seam metal) in strong structural condition or scheduled for near-term re-roofing.

02 / Behind-The-Meter Load

High Daytime Power Demand

Manufacturing, cold storage, distribution, and commercial facilities seeking direct on-site generation to offset utility kWh and demand charges.

03 / Community Solar Land

10 to 40+ Contiguous Acres

Cleared, flat parcels located within 1 mile of three-phase utility distribution lines and substations with available feeder hosting capacity.

04 / Co-Development & Acquisitions

Early-to-Mid Stage Pipelines

We partner with local originators, EPCs, and real estate brokers to co-develop or acquire commercial solar projects from site control through NTP.
